# SHATTERED DAD ADVENTURE
## Game Design Reference Document v0.1

---

## PREMISE

**Shattered Dad Adventure** is a tactical, turn-based roguelike about a middle-aged father descending into the most dangerous place known to mankind: a home improvement store on a Saturday.

You are Dad.

Armed with a fading metabolism, questionable knees, and an unwavering belief that "I can fix that myself," you must descend through 25 increasingly dangerous levels of suburban chaos to recover the legendary artifact:

> **The Remote of Ultimate Authority**
>
> Many have sought it. None have survived Vastco on a holiday weekend.

The tone combines: classic fantasy RPGs, roguelike dungeon crawlers, dad stereotypes, workplace humor, family chaos, mild absurdism, slapstick comedy, and adventure parody. The world should feel simultaneously epic and completely ridiculous. Every dad genuinely believes he is the hero. The universe strongly disagrees.

---

## CORE GAMEPLAY LOOP

1. **Descend** through a multi-level procedurally generated dungeon
2. **Gather** equipment and consumables across 25 floors
3. **Defeat bosses** at floors 5, 10, 15, 20, and 25
4. **Retrieve** the Remote of Ultimate Authority
5. **Escape alive** (most runs end in failure)

Every run is procedurally generated. No two disasters are exactly alike.

## DUNGEON STRUCTURE

100 floors across 10 themed biomes (10 floors each). Each floor is procedurally generated. Boss floors at every 10th floor (10, 20, 30 … 100). See **LEVEL_DESIGN.md** for full floor-by-floor narrative and enemy breakdown.

### Floor Structure Within Each Theme
- Floors X1–X4: Subtheme A
- Floors X5–X8: Subtheme B
- Floor X9: Pre-boss corridor / escalation
- Floor X0: Boss floor

### Biome Overview

| Floors | Theme | Main Setting | Subtheme A | Subtheme B | Boss |
|--------|-------|-------------|------------|------------|------|
| 1–10 | The House | Dad's home | The Garage | House Interior | The Garage (As Entity) |
| 11–20 | Lowe & Behold Hardware | Hardware store | Parking Lot | Store Interior | The Floor Manager |
| 21–30 | The Park | Community park | Sports Fields | Farmers Market / Common Area | The Farmers Market Coordinator |
| 31–40 | Vastco | Warehouse store | Parking Lot | Store Interior | The Membership Desk Guardian |
| 41–50 | The Soccer Complex | Tournament complex | Tournament Fields | Stands & Sidelines | The Tournament Coordinator |
| 51–60 | The School | Middle school | Athletic Wing / Gym | PTA Sector | The PTA President |
| 61–70 | T.G.I. Whenever's | Casual dining | Waiting Area / Lobby | Dining Room | The Hostess of Infinite Wait |
| 71–80 | IDKEA | Furniture labyrinth | The Showroom | Warehouse / Self-Serve | The Furniture Assembly Nightmare |
| 81–90 | The Neighborhood | Subdivision / cul-de-sac | Streets & Driveways | Cul-de-Sac & Backyard | The Perfect Neighbor |
| 91–100 | The HOA Dominion | Community center | Common Areas | HOA Inner Sanctum | The HOA President (Final Boss) |

## BOSSES

*Full boss mechanics in LEVEL_DESIGN.md. Summary below.*

### Floor 10 — The Garage (As Entity)
**Phases:** Avalanche → Inventory Check → Structural Concern
**Weakness:** Knowing where you put things (nobody does)

### Floor 20 — The Floor Manager (Lowe & Behold)
**Attacks:** The Greeting, Upsell Cascade, Project Scope Expansion, "While We're Back Here"
**Weakness:** Saying "I'm just getting screws" before engagement

### Floor 30 — The Farmers Market Coordinator
**Attacks:** Clipboard Intercept, Event Conflict, Volunteer Enrollment, "We Actually Need You To..."
**Weakness:** Claiming to be a vendor

### Floor 40 — The Membership Desk Guardian (Vastco)
**Attacks:** System Issue, Upgrade Offer, Return Dispute, "I'll Need to Call a Manager"
**Weakness:** None identified; encounter is mandatory

### Floor 50 — The Tournament Coordinator (Soccer)
**Attacks:** Schedule Revision, Volunteer Ask, Last-Minute Venue Change, "Per the Email We Sent"
**Weakness:** Having actually read the email (Tech Dad passive)

### Floor 60 — The PTA President
**Attacks:** The Warm Welcome, The Opportunity, The Guilt Architecture, "Your Kids Will Remember This"
**Weakness:** Arriving after the meeting has started

### Floor 70 — The Hostess of Infinite Wait
**Attacks:** Exit Narrowing, "Are You Leaving?" Check, Feedback Request Survey
**Weakness:** Paying via app

### Floor 80 — The Furniture Assembly Nightmare (IDKEA)
**Phases:** Parts Inventory → Instruction Interpretation → Missing Piece Reveal → Wrong Screw → Allen Wrench Crisis
**Weakness:** Owning The Correct Allen Wrench (Legendary item)

### Floor 90 — The Perfect Neighbor
**Attacks:** Conversational Intercept, Lawn Comment, Unsolicited Tip, "While You're Out"
**Weakness:** Yard Dad's Grass Surveillance counter-attack

### Floor 100 — The HOA President (Final Boss) — Karen Blackwell
**Phases:** The Welcome → The Citation → The Negotiation → "Per My Previous Email" → The Resolution
**Legendary Attack:** "Per My Previous Email" — unavoidable 30 Patience damage, unblockable
**Phase 3 Resolution:** Class-specific — each class resolves the negotiation differently (see LEVEL_DESIGN.md)
**Charm Resolution:** Gym Dad only. Karen has questions about protein. The gallon jug opens a door that no other class can find.
**Loot:** The Remote of Ultimate Authority (Mythic artifact)

**Class Resolution Summary:**
| Class | Resolution Method | Outcome |
|-------|------------------|---------|
| Grillmaster | Offer to cater the Annual Community Picnic | Remote + binding picnic commitment |
| DIY Dad | Fix the dripping faucet (60/35/5 roll) | Remote + variable consequences |
| Sports Dad | "Back In My Day" triggers nostalgia damage inward | Remote + Karen feels something |
| Tech Dad | HC-7 form never filed — confiscation procedurally void | Remote + policy update |
| Yard Dad | Satellite imagery + rainfall data disprove citation | Revised citation + Remote |
| Tactical Dad | Pre-identified statutory violation, prepared contingency | Remote + Karen's note |
| Gym Dad | Protein conversation. Chair yoga. BCAA offer. | Remote + "Dad Fit" Saturday class |
| Granddad | "I Told You So" — was right in 2019, was right in 2021 | Remote, no discussion required |

## ENDGAME

After defeating the HOA President and recovering the Remote of Ultimate Authority, the player must make the perilous ascent back through all 25 floors.

Many heroes successfully retrieve the artifact.

Very few make it home before somebody asks:

> *"Since you're up, can you help me with something?"*

At which point the true final boss battle begins.

## NARRATIVE THREAD

**Inciting Incident:** Dad's wife leaves for a girls' weekend. Her parting text: fix the dryer. The dryer runs on the Remote of Ultimate Authority — Dad's smart home controller. The Remote is missing. The Remote was left at the HOA Community Center two weeks ago. The garage door won't open without it. The car is in the garage. The thermostat has defaulted to 81 degrees.

**The Chain of Causation:**
1. Must escape garage → retrieves car manually
2. Needs dryer parts → drives to Lowe & Behold Hardware
3. En route home → daughter's cleats are at the park
4. Near park → Vastco list from wife
5. Vastco exit → son texts, tournament is TODAY
6. Soccer complex → team meeting migrates to the school
7. School → PTA ambush
8. Hunger → family restaurant
9. Restaurant is next to IDKEA → shelf wife wanted
10. Home finally → neighborhood is in chaos, HOA citation on mailbox → confrontation inevitable
11. HOA Community Center → final confrontation with Karen Blackwell → Remote recovered

**Endgame:** Dryer fixed. Garage opens. Thermostat at 72. Dad sits in recliner.
A voice from upstairs: *"Daaad? Since you're up, can you help me with something?"*

**True Final Boss:** The Request From Upstairs. Cannot be defeated. Can only be survived.

## BUILD CHANGELOG — v0.6.0 (2026-06-16)

Implemented from `SHATTERED DAD Adventure notes.txt`:

- **Interactable doors.** Bumping a closed door opens a numbered menu (Open / Use key / Pick lock / Kick down / Do nothing), with options built from class + inventory context.
- **DIY lockpicking.** DIY Dad can pick locks; success chance scales with hero level and inversely with dungeon depth (clamped 10–95%).
- **Gym door-kick.** Gym Dad can kick any door (even locked) open for minor self-damage (2–4 HP, never lethal).
- **Unidentified gear.** Found/shop gear hides its affixes (Upgrade / Learning Experience / no effect) and rarity until equipped or identified.
- **The Owner's Manual** (identify item) — reading it identifies all gear in inventory and equipped. On-theme replacement for an identify scroll.
- **The Cooler of Restoration** (healing fountain) — up to 3 per run, ≥10 floors apart; heals 40% maxHP + 8 over 3 charges.
- **Comedic enchantments & curses** — 10 new affixes including beneficial curses (Repulsive Beard → +2 PER, Haunted DIY Project → +1 Dadism power).
- **Inventory icons** — grid renders per-item pixel sprites instead of color blocks.
- **Scan pulse** — X emits an expanding ring showing the swept Perception radius.
- **Starting rations** — Grillmaster & Tactical Dad begin with extra food.
- **Freeze fix** — render loop is now crash-proof (try/catch + finite-coordinate guard), eliminating the "dad vanished / map frozen / inventory still works" class of freeze.

## BUILD CHANGELOG — v0.6.1 (2026-06-16)

- **Craftable & placeable traps (DIY / Tactical / Tech).** Three recipes — Duct Tape+Screws → Bagged Lego Minefield, Duct Tape+Plywood → Loose-Stair Rig, WD-40+9V Battery → Rigged Sprinkler. Carry as many as inventory allows; up to 5 of the same trap per slot. Place one per floor tile with **P** + direction; pursuing enemies that step on a placed trap spring it (damage + brief stun/root).
- **Bug fix: disappearing map.** `SPR_WATER` was referenced by the renderer but never defined, throwing whenever a water tile entered view. Added the sprite. (The v0.6.0 render hardening had already downgraded this from a hard freeze to a recoverable blank frame.)
