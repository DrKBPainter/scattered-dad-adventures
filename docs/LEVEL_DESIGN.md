# SHATTERED DAD ADVENTURE — LEVEL DESIGN & NARRATIVE FLOW
## 100 Floors | 10 Themes | A Saturday That Got Away From Him

---

## THE INCITING INCIDENT

It is Saturday, 7:43 AM.

Dad's wife left yesterday for a girls' weekend. Her parting words: *"The house is yours. Don't break anything."*

Her second parting words, delivered via text at 6:58 AM: *"While I'm gone can you fix the dryer? The dryer app says it's offline. You need the Remote."*

The Remote of Ultimate Authority — the universal smart home controller that Dad spent three weekends programming — controls the thermostat, the dryer, the garage door, the sprinkler system, the security cameras, and the television. Without it, the house is an unresponsive brick. The thermostat has defaulted to 81 degrees. The garage door will not open. The dryer will not start.

Dad last had the Remote at the HOA Community Meeting two weeks ago, where he was demonstrating that the Community Center's television — a 32-inch Blipco from 2011 — was "objectively unacceptable" and could be improved by simply pressing the correct sequence of inputs. He fell asleep during the treasurer's report. He left without the Remote.

The Remote is at the HOA Community Center.

The Community Center is across town.

The garage door won't open because the Remote isn't here.

Dad's car is in the garage.

The adventure has begun.

# THEME 1: THE HOUSE
**Floors 1–10**

*Dad has arrived at: His Own Home. He has lived here for eleven years. He has never felt more lost.*

The adventure does not begin in a dungeon. It begins in the kitchen, where Dad is holding his coffee and staring at the garage door with the expression of a man watching a storm form on the horizon.

The Remote must be retrieved. The car must be freed. The house must be traversed.

**Subtheme A — The Garage (Floors 1–4)**
The garage has not been fully entered since 2019. It is a biome unto itself. Items from three home improvement projects, two bikes nobody rides, a treadmill that is now a coat rack, and fourteen boxes of unknown origin form a treacherous labyrinth between Dad and the emergency garage release cord.

**Subtheme B — The House Interior (Floors 5–8)**
The car is out, but the morning has compounded. Dad cannot leave without: his wallet (kitchen counter, under a permission slip), his keys (jacket pocket, which jacket is unclear), his phone charger (bedroom, or possibly the car), and the list his wife texted him. Also the dog has gotten into something.

---

### Floor 1 — The Kitchen
*The campaign begins here, with coffee and rising dread.*

Dad reviews the situation. The Remote is gone. The house is warming up. The garage door is closed. Somewhere upstairs, a child is making a noise that suggests impending responsibility.

**Enemies:** Morning Obligation (slow, persistent), The Unread Text (spawns in groups)
**Trap:** The Permission Slip — requires a signature Dad does not remember agreeing to
**Loot:** Car Keys (wrong keys), Half-Charged Phone

---

### Floor 2 — The Hallway / Mud Room
*Every item left by every family member who is not currently home.*

A gauntlet of cleats, backpacks, and a hockey bag that has not moved since March. Somewhere under this is the correct jacket.

**Enemies:** Lost Shoe (must be found before proceeding), The Obligation Pile
**Trap:** The Shoe Pile — movement speed halved until cleared
**Loot:** Old Granola Bar (restores minor Energy), Permission Slip (unsigned, due Monday)

---

### Floor 3 — The Garage Threshold
*Dad has opened the door to the garage. He now deeply regrets opening the door to the garage.*

The garage is not a storage room. The garage is a decision museum — every tool purchased, every project started, and every object too large to throw away but too broken to use, preserved in permanent limbo.

**Enemies:** The Avalanche Stack (disturbing any box triggers a cascade), Mystery Box (contents unknown, possibly alive)
**Trap:** The Lego Minefield — catastrophic foot damage
**Loot:** Flashlight (dead batteries), Duct Tape Roll (partial)

---

### Floor 4 — The Garage Depths
*The emergency release cord is visible. It is seventeen feet away. It might as well be in another country.*

Dad must navigate past: the treadmill, three ladders (none the right size), a kayak nobody has used, an air compressor, and the shelf unit that is "definitely staying up fine."

**Enemies:** Rogue Rolling Cart, Shelf Unit (unstable, hostile)
**Trap:** The Kayak — blocks entire corridor, takes two turns to move, makes a noise
**Boss Room Unlock:** Pull the emergency release cord → Garage door opens → Car is freed
**Loot:** Pocket Knife, Bungee Cords (3, assorted)

---

### Floor 5 — The Living Room
*The car is free. But Dad cannot leave. The house needs to be secured first. This will take five minutes.*

The dog is in the living room. The dog has found something. The remote for the regular TV is also missing — a separate, lesser remote, but still concerning.

**Enemies:** The Dog (friendly, chaotic, a hazard), The Couch Cushion Formation (items buried within)
**Trap:** The Recliner Trap — if Dad sits down, productivity ends for remainder of day
**Loot:** $4.73 in loose change, The Lesser Remote (common quality)

---

### Floor 6 — The Upstairs Hallway
*Why is Dad upstairs? He came up here for something. The reason has been lost.*

**Enemies:** The Forgotten Errand (must be recalled), The Open Bathroom Faucet (discovered mid-floor)
**Trap:** The School Email Trap — phone buzzes, three new obligations spawn
**Loot:** Reading Glasses (Dad did not know he needed these)

---

### Floor 7 — The Attic
*The attic contains the answer to a question nobody remembers asking.*

Dad has come up here because he is "pretty sure" there is a backup garage opener in a box he labeled "MISC GARAGE" in 2017. There is no such box. There is a box labeled "MISC" and a box labeled "GARAGE (OLD)" and a box that is damp and should not be touched.

**Enemies:** The Mystery Box (contents hostile), The Ancient Christmas Decoration (aggressive, tangled)
**Trap:** The Low Beam — deals guaranteed head damage on entry
**Loot:** Old Garage Opener (wrong frequency), Nostalgic Item (triggers Dad's Memory passive — brief morale boost)

---

### Floor 8 — The Master Bedroom
*Everything Dad needs is in this room. None of it is where it should be.*

Wallet located. Keys located. Phone charger located. The correct jacket is located. Dad is now ready to leave. He has been ready to leave for forty-five minutes.

**Enemies:** The Charging Cable Knot (must be detangled), Second Phone (whose is this)
**Trap:** The Mirror — triggers self-assessment, costs 1 Patience
**Loot:** Correct Jacket, Wallet (partially funded)

---

### Floor 9 — The Driveway
*Dad is outside. The car is out. The journey could begin. The neighbor has emerged.*

The neighbor has noticed Dad in the driveway and is now walking over with the energy of someone who has been waiting for exactly this opportunity for several days.

**Enemies:** The Neighbor (initiates conversation, cannot be interrupted politely), The Delivery Package (must be brought inside before departing)
**Trap:** The Friendly Detour — neighbor mentions "one quick thing" which costs 2 turns
**Status Effect:** Neighborly Obligation (persists until escaped)

---

### Floor 10 — BOSS: THE GARAGE (AS ENTITY)
*It was never just a room. It was a final exam.*

The garage door is open, the car is out — but Dad has realized he left the Vastco list inside and must re-enter. The Garage has now fully awakened. Every object that was teetering is now teetering more urgently. The Garage manifests its will.

**BOSS: The Garage**
- **Phase 1 — The Avalanche:** Periodic waves of falling items. Dodge or receive impact damage.
- **Phase 2 — The Inventory Check:** Must locate one specific item among forty-seven items. Wrong guesses trigger additional avalanches.
- **Phase 3 — The Structural Concern:** The shelf unit reveals itself as unstable. Dad must decide whether to fix it now (costs 3 turns, saves future problem) or leave (spawns the "shelf problem" as a recurring debuff in later floors).
- **Victory:** Dad escapes the garage with the list, the car, and his dignity mostly intact.

**Loot on Defeat:**
- The Vastco List (7 items, will become 14 by the time he arrives)
- Garage Door Opener (functional, for the wrong car)

# THEME 2: LOWE'S HARDWARE
**Floors 11–20**

*Dad has arrived at: The Hardware Store. It is 9:15 AM on a Saturday. The parking lot is already a war.*

Every man who enters Lowe & Behold on a Saturday morning believes he will be there for fifteen minutes. This has never happened once in recorded history. The Hardware Store is a place where time behaves differently, projects multiply on contact with new materials, and the correct screw size is never in stock.

**Subtheme A — The Parking Lot (Floors 11–14)**
The parking lot is not a parking lot. It is a social landscape, a conflict zone, and a reflection of society. Contractors park at angles. Trucks idle indefinitely. The cart return is thirty-seven feet from where anyone parks.

**Subtheme B — The Store Interior (Floors 15–18)**
Inside, the store is organized according to a logic that was clear when it was designed and has since become theoretical. The aisle numbering system is advisory. The signage refers to departments that were reorganized in 2021.

---

### Floor 15 — The Lumber Aisle
*Dad needs screws. The screws are near the lumber. The lumber is everywhere.*

The lumber aisle is the widest aisle in the store and still feels narrow because every cart is loaded with twelve-foot boards extending perpendicular to the direction of travel.

**Enemies:** The Lumber Cart (unwieldy, controlled by someone who cannot see around it), The Wood Splinter (applies ongoing damage if contact is made with any board)

### Floor 16 — The Fastener Aisle
*There are 847 types of screw. Dad needs one specific type. The aisle is organized by a system that requires a degree to understand.*

**Enemies:** The Analysis Paralysis (a status effect that descends when Dad reads the fourth different screw packaging), The Bin Avalanche (loose fasteners everywhere)
**Trap:** The Wrong Screw — purchased with confidence, discovered incorrect at home, requires return trip
**Loot:** The Correct Screw (rare drop, 20% chance), Reading Glasses (Dad forgot his)

---

### Floor 17 — The Appliance Parts Section
*The dryer needs a part. The part might be here. The parts are organized by appliance brand in a system that assumes Dad knows the model number of his dryer.*

**Enemies:** The Model Number Void (Dad cannot remember the model number, must call home, child answers, child also does not know)
**Trap:** The Universal Part — claims compatibility with all models, compatible with none
**Loot:** Dryer Belt (possibly correct), Phone Stand (impulse purchase)

---

### Floor 18 — The Tool Display
*Dad does not need new tools. Dad has all the tools. Dad is now holding a drill that feels different from his drill.*

**Enemies:** The Upgraded Version (renders Dad's existing tool emotionally obsolete), The Display Model (cannot be purchased, exists only to create desire)
**Trap:** The Tool Upgrade — Dad buys a new tool instead of fixing the dryer, costs significant gold
**Loot:** Lithium Battery Pack (compatible with nothing he owns), Level (Dad already owns three)

---

### Floor 19 — The Self-Checkout / Return Desk
*Dad has found the part. Now he must survive the purchase.*

The self-checkout machine has opinions about the weight of items in the bagging area. The return desk has a line that has existed since the store opened. The receipt is required.

**Enemies:** The Self-Checkout Machine (hostile, unpredictable, calls for assistance every third scan), The Return Line (static, immovable)
**Trap:** The Unexpected Item in Bagging Area — freezes all progress until resolved by an employee who is helping someone else
**Loot:** Lowe & Behold Rewards Points (accrued, unredeemable, forgotten)

---

### Floor 20 — BOSS: THE FLOOR MANAGER
*He appeared from nowhere. He knows where everything is. He will upsell Dad into a better version of what he needed.*

The Floor Manager is a figure of terrifying competence who can locate any item in the store within thirty seconds and will spend the next twelve minutes explaining why Dad should have bought the premium version.

**BOSS: The Floor Manager**
- **Phase 1 — The Greeting:** Instantly approaches. Resistance is futile. "Can I help you find anything today?"
- **Phase 2 — The Upsell Cascade:** For every item Dad mentions needing, the Floor Manager presents a superior, more expensive version. Each acceptance deals financial damage. Each rejection requires a Wisdom check.
- **Phase 3 — The Project Scope Expansion:** The Floor Manager realizes Dad's project is "actually a bigger job than you think" and begins outlining what the project *should* be. Dad's quest log gains two new items.
- **Legendary Attack — "While We're Back Here":** Dad is walked to the farthest corner of the store. By the time they return, 45 minutes have passed.
- **Weakness:** Saying "I'm just getting screws" before engagement begins. Reduces encounter to 2 turns.

**Loot on Defeat:**
- The Correct Part (guaranteed)
- New Project Seed (starts a side quest that will not be completed this weekend)
- Military Discount Applied (10% off — Dad is not military, Floor Manager did not ask)

# THEME 3: THE PARK
**Floors 21–30**

*Dad has arrived at: Riverside Community Park. Something is happening here. Many things are happening here.*

The park is not the park Dad remembers. The park Dad remembers was a quiet rectangle of grass with a swing set. This park has become a weekend ecosystem: farmers market, 5K fun run, youth soccer, dog show, and a birthday party that has claimed the main pavilion and is expanding.

**Subtheme A — The Sports Fields (Floors 21–24)**
Six soccer fields, three baseball diamonds, and one lacrosse field that nobody uses but the field maintenance guy mows it anyway because it's on his route. Finding one pair of cleats somewhere in this complex is an archaeological undertaking.

**Subtheme B — The Farmers Market / Common Area (Floors 25–28)**
The market has taken over the park's central plaza. Artisanal vendors, a beekeeper, a man selling homemade hot sauce with names like "Dad Regrets," and a woman who has been here since dawn and has achieved complete spatial dominance.

# THEME 4: COSTCO
**Floors 31–40**

*Dad has arrived at: Vastco. The parking lot is its own biome. Many who enter do not return with fewer items than intended.*

Vastco is not a store. Vastco is a philosophy. A way of life. A place where the rational decision is always to buy the 48-pack because the per-unit cost is undeniable. Dad has come for seventeen items. He will leave with thirty-one, a new set of tires he didn't plan for, and a food court hot dog that costs $1.50 and remains the most economically stable purchase in America.

**Subtheme A — The Parking Lot (Floors 31–34)**
The Vastco parking lot operates on its own physics. Carts the size of small vehicles. Turns that require three-point execution. The inexplicable traffic light at the exit that causes philosophical despair.

**Subtheme B — The Store Interior (Floors 35–38)**
The interior is a warehouse cathedral. Ceilings thirty feet high. Pallets of goods stacked to dangerous heights. Samples arranged to maximize involuntary deviation from the shopping path.

### Floor 40 — BOSS: THE MEMBERSHIP DESK GUARDIAN
*She controls the front desk. She controls returns. She controls membership renewals. She controls Dad.*

**BOSS: The Membership Desk Guardian**

- **Phase 2 — The Upgrade Offer:** His current membership level is Basic. For $60 more, he gets Executive, with 2% cashback. The Guardian presents the math. The math is compelling.
